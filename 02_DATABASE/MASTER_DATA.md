# MASTER DATA

Version: 1.0

Master Data governs all configurable balance. Business logic must not contain balance values.

Categories include Exercise, exercise difficulty/reward, heroes, stats/roles, equipment/type/grade/set, monsters, bosses, tower, quests, achievements, titles, buildings, decorations, pets, affinity, notifications, AI messages, loading and health tips.

Exercise definitions contain code, display name, category, intensity, base EXP, multiplier, duration range, and stat focus. Difficulty multipliers and equipment drop grades are data-driven.

Equipment is earned only through tower, quest, boss, season, event, or drop—not purchased with points. Points fund guild and cosmetic personalization.

All additions and balance revisions are versioned Master Data changes.
