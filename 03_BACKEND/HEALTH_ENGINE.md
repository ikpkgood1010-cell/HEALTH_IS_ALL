# HEALTH ENGINE

Version: 1.0

---

# Purpose

The Health Engine is the core domain of Guild Health.

Its responsibility is to evaluate the Guild Master's health behaviors, encourage sustainable healthy habits, and provide meaningful feedback.

Game systems must consume Health Engine results, not replace them.

Health always has higher priority than gameplay.

---

# Core Philosophy

Health First.

The purpose of Guild Health is not to maximize gameplay.

The purpose is to help users build sustainable healthy habits.

Game mechanics exist only to support healthy behavior.

---

# Health Domains

The Health Engine evaluates the following domains.

- Exercise
- Daily Activity
- Walking
- Sleep
- Hydration
- Nutrition
- Recovery
- Body Composition
- Consistency
- Mental Wellness (Future)

---

# Daily Health Evaluation

Every day, the engine generates a Daily Health Summary.

The summary contains:

- Health Score
- Exercise Summary
- Recovery Summary
- Sleep Summary
- Nutrition Summary
- Hydration Summary
- Daily Achievement
- Suggested Improvements

---

# Weekly Evaluation

Every week:

- Weekly Trend
- Habit Consistency
- Recovery Balance
- Exercise Balance
- Recommended Goals

---

# Monthly Evaluation

Every month:

- Habit Growth
- Personal Bests
- Exercise Diversity
- Recovery Quality
- Long-term Progress

---

# Health Score

Health Score represents overall healthy behavior.

It is calculated using configurable rules.

Health Score must never encourage unhealthy overtraining.

---

# Recovery Protection

If recovery is insufficient:

- Reduce bonus rewards.
- Encourage rest.
- Display recovery advice.
- Never punish the user.

---

# AI Coaching

The Health Engine provides structured information to AI.

AI generates:

- Daily coaching
- Recovery advice
- Motivation
- Educational content

AI must never diagnose disease.

AI provides wellness guidance only.

---

# Trust Integration

Healthy consistency increases Companion Trust.

Missing a day should not significantly reduce Trust.

Returning after a break should be positively reinforced.

---

# RPG Integration

The RPG system consumes:

- Health Score
- Completed Activities
- Weekly Consistency

The RPG system must never override Health Engine decisions.

---

# Future Expansion

Support:

- Smart Watch Integration
- Wearable Devices
- Blood Pressure
- Blood Sugar
- HRV
- VO2 Max

without architectural redesign.
