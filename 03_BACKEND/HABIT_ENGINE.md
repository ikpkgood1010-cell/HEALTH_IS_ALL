# HABIT ENGINE

Version: 1.0

---

# Purpose

The Habit Engine is the core behavioral analysis system of Guild Health.

Its mission is to measure, encourage, and strengthen sustainable healthy habits.

It evaluates consistency rather than intensity.

The Habit Engine is a health coaching system, not a gamification system.

---

# Core Philosophy

Healthy habits are built through repetition.

Consistency is more valuable than perfection.

Returning after a break is always encouraged.

Small improvements matter.

---

# Habit Domains

The engine evaluates:

- Exercise
- Walking
- Sleep
- Hydration
- Nutrition
- Recovery
- Stretching
- Body Composition
- Weekly Consistency
- Monthly Consistency

---

# Habit Score

Each domain receives a score.

Overall Habit Score is calculated using configurable rules.

The score ranges from 0 to 100.

---

# Habit Grade

Grades are determined by Habit Score.

S

A+

A

B+

B

C

D

The grading thresholds are configurable.

---

# Consistency Analysis

The engine evaluates:

Current Streak

Weekly Completion Rate

Monthly Completion Rate

Recovery Balance

Exercise Diversity

Routine Stability

---

# Positive Reinforcement

The engine rewards:

Returning after absence

Improved consistency

Balanced recovery

Healthy variety

Long-term improvement

The engine must never punish temporary inactivity.

---

# Recommendations

The engine generates recommendations based on behavioral patterns.

Examples:

Increase water intake.

Improve sleep consistency.

Add stretching twice a week.

Take a recovery day.

Try a different exercise this week.

---

# AI Integration

The Habit Engine provides structured data for AI coaching.

AI should explain why a recommendation was generated.

---

# RPG Integration

The RPG system may consume:

Habit Score

Habit Grade

Consistency Metrics

Long-term Improvement

The Habit Engine always has higher priority than RPG progression.

---

# Privacy

Habit analysis belongs only to the user unless explicit sharing is enabled.

Leaderboard participation must never expose private health details.
