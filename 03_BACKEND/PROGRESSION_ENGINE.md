# PROGRESSION ENGINE

Version: 1.0

---

# Purpose

The Progression Engine defines how the Guild Master's long-term growth is achieved.

Progression represents healthy habit formation over time.

RPG mechanics visualize progress but never replace healthy behavior.

---

# Core Philosophy

Progress reflects consistency.

The goal is not to reward extreme effort.

The goal is to encourage sustainable healthy habits.

Users should always feel that returning after a break is worthwhile.

---

# Progression Sources

Progress may be earned from:

- Exercise
- Walking
- Recovery
- Healthy Consistency
- Weekly Goals
- Monthly Goals
- Health Score
- Special Health Events

---

# Progression Systems

The engine manages:

- Level
- Experience (EXP)
- Points
- Companion Trust
- Tower Progress
- Equipment Growth
- Titles
- Achievements

---

# Experience (EXP)

EXP represents accumulated healthy effort.

EXP is granted through Rule Engine calculations.

EXP values must remain configurable.

---

# Level

Levels represent long-term growth.

Levels never decrease.

Users should always feel permanent progress.

---

# Points

Points are earned when leveling up.

Points are used for:

- Guild decoration
- Companion customization
- UI themes
- Titles
- Cosmetic rewards

Points must never increase equipment power.

---

# Companion Trust

Trust grows through healthy consistency.

Trust should increase slowly over time.

Short breaks should have minimal impact.

Returning after a long break should trigger positive encouragement.

---

# Tower Progress

Tower progress represents personal journey.

Tower progression depends primarily on consistency rather than difficulty.

Missing days should delay progress but never erase achievements.

---

# Equipment

Equipment symbolizes growth.

Equipment strength is determined by activity history.

Equipment is never purchased with Points.

Inactive equipment may become rusty after long inactivity.

Rust can be removed through renewed activity.

---

# Achievement Philosophy

Achievements celebrate milestones.

They should recognize:

- Consistency
- Recovery
- Variety
- Personal improvement
- Long-term dedication

---

# Anti-Overtraining Rules

The engine must never reward unhealthy behavior.

Recovery and balance should always influence progression.

---

# Future Expansion

Support:

- Seasonal progression
- Guild progression
- Family progression
- Corporate wellness programs

without architectural redesign.
