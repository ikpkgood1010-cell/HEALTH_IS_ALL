# QUEST ENGINE

Version: 1.0

---

# Purpose

The Quest Engine transforms healthy behaviors into motivating daily, weekly, and long-term missions.

Quests exist to encourage healthy habits.

They are not mandatory objectives.

Users should never feel punished for missing quests.

---

# Core Philosophy

Health First.

Quests guide healthy behavior.

Progress is celebrated.

Failure is never punished.

---

# Adaptive Missions

The engine should adapt mission difficulty.

Example:

Beginner

↓

Walk 20 minutes

Experienced User

↓

Walk 60 minutes

Recovery Needed

↓

Stretch 15 minutes

---

# Healthy Balance Rules

The engine must never generate missions that encourage overtraining.

Recovery and sleep should be considered before assigning exercise missions.

---

# Rewards

Mission rewards may include:

EXP

Points

Titles

Cosmetic Unlocks

Companion Trust

Tower Progress

Health Insights

---

# Streak Philosophy

Streaks encourage consistency.

Breaking a streak should never create strong negative emotions.

Returning after a break should feel rewarding.

---

# Community Missions

Examples:

Guild members complete 1,000 km together.

Drink 10,000 L of water as a community.

Complete 5,000 recovery sessions.

Community missions encourage cooperation rather than competition.

---

# Hidden Missions

Hidden missions reward exploration.

Example:

Complete three different exercises in one week.

Sleep over 8 hours for five consecutive nights.

---

# AI Integration

The AI Coach may suggest personalized missions.

Administrators can enable or disable AI-generated missions.
