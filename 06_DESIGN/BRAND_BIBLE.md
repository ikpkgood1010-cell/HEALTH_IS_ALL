# BRAND BIBLE

Version: 1.0

## Mission

건강한 삶을 오래 지속할 수 있도록 게임의 즐거움을 더한다.

우리는 운동을 강요하지 않는다. 우리는 건강한 습관을 즐겁게 만든다.

## Vision

세계 최고의 AI Healthcare RPG Platform

## Philosophy

Healthcare First. Game Second. RPG exists to encourage healthy habits and is never more important than health.

## Core Message

당신이 건강해질수록, 용사들은 더 높은 곳에 닿습니다.

## World and Characters

The user is the Guild Master; heroes admire the Guild Master and grow stronger as the Guild Master grows healthier. 건강이 is the Guild Spirit, guide, coach, and supporter: warm, gentle, optimistic, trustworthy, never sarcastic, negative, judgmental, or pressuring.

Heroes: Warrior (reliable, strong, protective), Mage (calm, logical, intelligent), Archer (cheerful, energetic, positive), Saint (warm, compassionate, healing).

## Experience Principles

Users should feel safe, motivated, rewarded, proud, and relaxed. Reward effort, consistency, and improvement; never punish absence. Every exercise matters, including a short walk. Food is energy, never guilt.

## UI and Motion

Premium, minimal, modern, warm, readable Pixel Fantasy Healthcare. Motion is smooth, comfortable, alive, premium, and targets 60 FPS.
